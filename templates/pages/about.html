The Organisation

About NUJ India

The National Union of Journalists (India) — commonly known as NUJ(I) — is one of India's foremost organizations representing the interests of working journalists across the country. Established to uphold the principles of press freedom, professional ethics, and the dignity of the journalism profession, NUJ India has been a steadfast voice for journalists for over six decades.

NUJ India is a proud affiliate member of the International Federation of Journalists (IFJ), Brussels, Belgium — the world's largest organization of journalists, representing more than 600,000 journalists in over 140 countries. This affiliation connects Indian journalists to the global movement for press freedom and journalists' rights.

The organization works tirelessly to defend journalists from harassment, threats, and unjust termination; advocates for fair wages and working conditions; promotes professional training and development; and strengthens solidarity among media workers across all platforms — print, broadcast, and digital.

Uttar Pradesh Chapter

NUJ Uttar Pradesh

The Uttar Pradesh chapter of NUJ India represents thousands of journalists working across all 75 districts of UP — from Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, and Agra to the remotest districts like Shravasti, Sonbhadra, and Lalitpur.

The UP chapter maintains City Units in each district, led by elected office bearers including a President, Secretary, Vice President, Treasurer, and Executive Members. These city units serve as the ground-level representatives of NUJ UP, providing journalists with a local support structure, legal assistance when needed, and a community of peers.

NUJ UP actively monitors press freedom violations in the state, intervenes when journalists face threats or police harassment, and engages with government authorities to ensure the safety and professional security of media workers.

Our Work

Key Activities

🛡️

Press Freedom Defence

Intervening on behalf of journalists facing harassment, FIRs, threats, or wrongful termination by employers or authorities.

🤝

Membership & Solidarity

Building a strong network of journalists across UP who support each other professionally and in times of crisis.

📚

Training & Capacity Building

Workshops, seminars, and training programs for journalists to upgrade their skills in digital journalism, ethics, and legal literacy.

📢

Advocacy & Policy

Engaging with state and central government on policies affecting the media industry — from wage boards to working conditions.

🌐

IFJ Global Network

As an IFJ affiliate, members can access international solidarity, resources, and professional networks across 140+ countries.

🏙️

City Units Across UP

Active presence in all 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh through elected City Units with local office bearers.
