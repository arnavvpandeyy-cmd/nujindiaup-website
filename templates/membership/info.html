{# Eligibility #}

Eligibility Criteria

  • Working journalist employed in print, digital, broadcast, or news agency
  • Freelance journalists with verifiable press credentials
  • Indian citizen currently residing and working in India
  • Must hold a valid press card or letter of appointment from a recognised media organisation
